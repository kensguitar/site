Ken’s Guitar Studio

Guitar Lessons in Chilliwack, BC

Hi, my name is Ken Klassen, and I love to pass on the talent of playing the guitar. I've taught many guitarists over my 40 years on the instrument, and I'm currently accepting new students at my studio in Chilliwack, BC.

I started playing guitar at age 14, and my heroes were B.B. King, Eric Clapton, Carlos Santana, and Jimi Hendrix. While I was initially drawn to blues and rock, I now play and teach country, folk, gospel, and other genres as well.

I am currently winding down my business, but now offer a 6 week introductory course for mature students. You will learn all the tools you need in order to play the songs you love.
